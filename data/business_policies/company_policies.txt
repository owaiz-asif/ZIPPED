COMPANY POLICIES AND PROCEDURES - 2024

WORK FROM HOME POLICY:
- Employees can work remotely up to 3 days per week
- Core hours: 10:00 AM - 3:00 PM EST (must be available)
- Home office setup reimbursement: up to $500
- Internet stipend: $50/month

TIME OFF POLICY:
- Paid Time Off (PTO): 20 days per year
- Sick Leave: 10 days per year
- Holidays: 10 company holidays
- PTO requests: Submit at least 2 weeks in advance
- Blackout dates: Last 2 weeks of each quarter

MEETING ETIQUETTE:
- All meetings must have an agenda
- Meetings should start and end on time
- Maximum meeting duration: 60 minutes (unless approved)
- No meetings on Fridays after 2:00 PM
- Standup meetings: Maximum 15 minutes

COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES:
- Response time: Within 4 business hours
- Urgent matters: Use Slack @channel or call
- Email: For formal communications and documentation
- Slack: For quick questions and team coordination
- Documentation: All decisions must be documented

CODE REVIEW PROCESS:
- All code must be reviewed before merging
- Minimum 1 approval required
- Reviews should be completed within 24 hours
- Automated tests must pass before merge
- Documentation required for complex changes

ONBOARDING PROCESS:
- Week 1: Orientation and tool setup
- Week 2: Team introductions and project overview
- Week 3: First assigned tasks with mentor
- Week 4: Full integration into team workflow
- 30-day check-in: Feedback session with manager

PERFORMANCE REVIEWS:
- Frequency: Quarterly
- Format: Self-assessment + manager review
- Focus: Goals, achievements, growth areas
- Action items: Development plan for next quarter
- Compensation review: Annually in Q4

EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT:
- Submit expenses within 30 days
- Receipts required for all expenses over $25
- Approval required for expenses over $500
- Reimbursement processing: 5-7 business days

TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT:
- Annual training budget: $2,000 per employee
- Conference attendance: Up to 2 per year
- Online courses: Company-paid subscriptions available
- Internal training: Monthly tech talks and workshops

EQUIPMENT POLICY:
- Laptop refresh: Every 3 years
- Monitor and accessories: Provided by company
- Software licenses: Company covers all work-related tools
- Personal device usage: Allowed with security approval

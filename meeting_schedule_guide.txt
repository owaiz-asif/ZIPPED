MEETING SCHEDULING BEST PRACTICES

Optimal Meeting Times Based on Energy Levels:
- Morning Peak (9:00 AM - 11:00 AM): Best for creative work, design reviews, and strategic planning. Energy levels are highest during this time.
- Mid-Morning (11:00 AM - 12:00 PM): Good for team standups and quick syncs. Still high energy but starting to decline.
- Afternoon (2:00 PM - 4:00 PM): Ideal for collaborative meetings, client calls, and problem-solving sessions. Energy recovers after lunch.
- Late Afternoon (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM): Suitable for wrap-up meetings and status updates. Energy starts declining.
- Avoid Early Morning (before 9 AM): Low energy, people are still waking up.
- Avoid Post-Lunch (12:00 PM - 1:30 PM): Energy dip after eating, people are less focused.

Meeting Duration Guidelines:
- Quick Sync: 15 minutes - Use for daily standups and brief updates
- Standard Meeting: 30 minutes - Most common meeting length
- Deep Dive: 60 minutes - For complex discussions and planning
- Extended Session: 90-120 minutes - For workshops and intensive planning

Meeting Types and Best Times:
1. Design Reviews: 10:00 AM - 11:00 AM (Peak creativity)
2. Team Standups: 9:30 AM or 2:00 PM (Avoid post-lunch dip)
3. Client Meetings: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM or 2:00 PM - 4:00 PM
4. One-on-Ones: Flexible, but 10:00 AM or 3:00 PM work best
5. Brainstorming: 10:00 AM - 11:30 AM (Peak creative energy)
6. Status Updates: 4:00 PM (End of day wrap-up)

Energy-Based Scheduling:
- High Energy Tasks: Schedule important meetings, creative work, and decision-making during 9-11 AM
- Medium Energy Tasks: Routine meetings, reviews, and collaborative work during 2-4 PM
- Low Energy Tasks: Administrative tasks, emails, and planning during low-energy periods

Meeting Best Practices:
- Schedule meetings at least 24 hours in advance
- Include clear agenda in calendar invite
- Limit meetings to essential participants
- End meetings 5 minutes early to allow transition time
- Avoid back-to-back meetings without breaks
- Schedule buffer time between meetings (15-30 minutes)
